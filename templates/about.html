About

An engineer who likes to know how things fail.

I trained as a marine engineer and graduated with First Class Honours from Nigeria Maritime University. Engine rooms teach you to respect failure modes: you learn how a system breaks before you trust it to run. I brought that habit into software.

Today I work across three areas. As a security engineer, I lead authorised API penetration tests against the OWASP API Security Top 10 and write reports that engineers can act on. In AI research, I fine-tune and evaluate open-weight language models. As an AI full-stack engineer, I build and deploy the platforms around them, from the FastAPI back end to the React front end and the cloud it runs on.

The three feed each other. Building products makes me a sharper tester, and testing other people's systems makes what I build harder to break.

How I work

Three principles.

01

Evidence over opinion

Every finding is reproducible and scored. Every claim about a model is backed by an evaluation.

02

Root causes, not symptoms

Twenty findings with one cause deserve one fix. I look for the shared cause so teams fix less and fix it properly.

03

Discretion by default

Client systems, data and findings stay confidential. I share outcomes, never details.
